# Statamic Offers — Marketplace

## Price

**Free with `statamic-funnels`, $29 on its own.** Decided by Adrian on 2026-08-25.

The reasoning: on its own it is a small thing — a table, a tag and one screen. Its value shows up
when something else asks it what a thing costs *here*, which today means the funnel addon and
tomorrow a shop. Pricing it like a full addon would tax the piece that makes the other ones work.

## What it is for

Order bumps and upsells need somewhere for a discounted price to live that a browser cannot reach.
That is the whole product. Everything else — the words, the slot, the counters — follows from having
a record at all.
